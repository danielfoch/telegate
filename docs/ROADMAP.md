# Community first. Convenience later.

**More life. Less screen.** Telegate starts with people who want to clone the repo, run their own infrastructure, connect their own agents and use their own phone.

| Offering | Status | Experience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Telegate Community | Open-source developer preview | Self-hosted relay, own OpenAI key, DIY iPhone build, own computers and harnesses. No Telegate software fee. |
| Full self-hosted push build | Included in source; requires Apple/APNs configuration | Users with the appropriate developer setup can enable push on their own infrastructure. |
| Managed Telegate app | Planned, not available | App Store installation, guided pairing, managed relay/notifications, updates and support. Pricing is undecided. Requires product maturity and Apple review. |

The community edition remains available. Commercial value should come from less installation and operational work. Do not describe a planned App Store app, global community network or managed endpoint as live.

## Release sequence

1. Make the fresh-clone → self-host → physical-iPhone route reproducible, including a no-APNs Personal Team scheme and honest setup requirements.
2. Get developers using real Codex/Claude sessions outside their office. Fix onboarding, interruption/recovery and destination-selection issues from those trials.
3. Validate live voice, Action Button, actual notifications, multiple computers and follow-ups on physical devices.
4. Offer a tested beta through the appropriate Apple distribution process, then submit a polished app for review. Approval and a launch date are not guaranteed.

Keep the brand focused on useful completed work and time for life. Avoid fabricated time savings, streak pressure, or forcing self-hosters into a hosted account. Each self-hosted relay owns its users, metrics and optional leaderboard.
